# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Potential clients, design agencies, tech startups, and recruiters looking for high-quality Product Design or Web Design expertise.

## Product Purpose
Showcase Sacca Dafia's portfolio, professional background, case studies (including Cactuce, eHadj, and Asset IQ), design methodology, and provide a direct path to book a discovery call.

## Brand Personality
Premium, minimalist, editorial, rigorous, and precise. A bold balance between a light-themed minimal editorial hero and a high-contrast dark cinematic green brand experience.

## Anti-references
Generic SaaS landing page clichés (purple-blue gradient overlays, nested card grids, overused geometric sans-serif typefaces like plain Inter, side-stripe card borders).

## Design Principles
- **Editorial Contrast**: Leverage striking transitions between spacious white columns and dense dark visual cases.
- **Process Transparency**: Highlight real project reasoning, wireframing, benchmarking, and QA follow-through rather than static templates.
- **Visual Restraint**: Focus on large deliberate typography, tinted near-blacks, and purposeful micro-animations.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast levels for text elements, fully responsive and fluid grids, and clean fallback options for systems with reduced-motion preferences.
